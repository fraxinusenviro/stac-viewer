STAC Viewer

LiDAR point clouds and rasters, satellite imagery, and their fusion, straight from public cloud storage.

NRCan CanElevation point clouds (COPC). The list follows the map.

LiDAR-derived rasters from NRCan HRDEM: the DTM (bare earth) and DSM (surface, incl. trees and buildings), per LiDAR project and as national mosaics. Each as hillshade, elevation or shaded elevation.

Sentinel-2 Level-2A from Element 84 Earth Search: band combinations, vegetation and water indices, scene classes.

LiDAR structure and Sentinel-2 spectra fused per pixel, for reading land cover: canopy height tells trees from grass and roofs from roads; the indices tell green from grey and water from shadow.
